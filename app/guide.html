01What is Viberquest

Most onchain games make you play a separate game. Here the game is the thing you already do: buying early, holding, launching, catching graduations. Viberquest reads what your wallet did on vibe/vibe and turns it into XP, loot and rankings.

TRADE

Buy, sell, launch and hold on vibe/vibe like you normally would.

QUEST

Daily, weekly and story quests fill up on their own as your trades land, usually within seconds.

FORGE

Quests drop materials. Forge them into gear that shows on your hero and boosts your XP.

COMPETE

Climb the weekly board, carry your vibe/vibe guild up the leagues, pick tokens in the Arena.

02Get started

  1. Get a wallet like MetaMask or Rabby with a little Robinhood Chain testnet ETH, the same setup you use on vibe/vibe.
  2. Open viberquest.fun and pick how you want in:
    • Just look: type your address or your @X handle (if it's on your vibe/vibe guild profile). You see your hero, quests and guild right away. Nothing to connect.
    • Play without connecting: the game gives you a small code amount, like 0.0002347 ETH. Send exactly that much ETH to your own address on Robinhood Chain testnet (it comes straight back to you, you only pay the network fee), or buy any token on vibe/vibe for that amount. The game unlocks by itself a few seconds later. You never sign anything on this site.
    • Connect wallet: sign one message to prove the wallet is yours. It is not a transaction, it costs nothing and gives the game no access to your funds.
  3. Read the quest board. You get 3 daily quests, 5 weekly quests and the first story chapter.
  4. Go trade on vibe/vibe. Keep the game open in another tab and watch the board light up. Trades need to be worth at least 0.002 ETH to count.
  5. Claim your rewards for XP and materials, then head to the Forge.
💡Quests only count trades made after you got the quest. Your older history still matters though: it decides your class and your starting level.

03The town

Everything happens on one pixel plaza, seen from above. Click anywhere to walk there, or use the arrow keys / WASD and press E at a door. On a phone, tap the plaza or the buttons under it. Day and night follow the real UTC time.

The town is live: everyone who's playing right now walks the same plaza with their own hero and gear, with their level and their @X handle (or short address) over their head. When someone trades on vibe/vibe a bubble pops over their head (BUY $TOKEN 0.05), and you can wave with emotes (GM, LFG, GG, 🔥, 🚀, 👋, keys 1 to 6). Click another player to see their hero card. This week's top players you don't see online walk around as faded ghosts.

04Your hero

Every wallet has its own pixel character, generated from the address, so nobody looks exactly like you. Your class comes from how you trade, your level from trading XP (your whole vibe/vibe history) plus quest XP (everything you claim here).

Your hero has a public page you can share on X with a big card of your character, level, gear and badges. Open the Hero window and press Share on X.

06Materials & forge

Quests and chests drop four materials: coppersilvergolddiamond. At the Forge you pick a tier and a slot, swing the hammer, and get a random piece of gear of that tier.

If the forge fails you get half of the materials back. Each point of power on equipped gear adds +1% XP to every quest reward, up to +50%. There are five slots: head, eyes, body, aura and pet (auras and pets start at rare).

Salvage: spare gear you don't wear can be melted back: common → 2 copper, rare → 2 silver, epic → 1 gold + 1 silver, legendary → 1 diamond.

07Daily chest

A chest waits on the street every UTC day: 2 to 4 copper for sure, a 25% chance of silver and a 5% chance of gold, plus 10 XP. It opens for wallets that traded on vibe/vibe in the last 7 days, so empty wallets can't farm it.

08Guild season

Viberquest uses the real vibe/vibe guilds. Join one on vibe/vibe and turn on "show me in the member list" so the game can see you. There's nothing to set up in the game.

  • Guild points = the quest XP the guild's players claim this week. One member adds at most 1,000 a week, so a single whale can't carry a guild.
  • Leagues: BronzeSilverGoldDiamond. Every Monday the top 20% of each league move up and the bottom 20% (and guilds that didn't score) move down.
  • Guild goal: the combined weekly volume of all visible members unlocks up to three chests for every Viberquest player in the guild.

10Seasons & badges

A season is one week, Monday 00:00 to Monday 00:00 UTC. When it ends the boards are archived in the Hall of Fame under Past seasons, and badges are handed out:

  • FAME #1, #2, #3: the top 3 questers of the week.
  • ARENA #1, #2, #3: the top 3 of the fantasy league.
  • GUILD CHAMP: every player of the #1 guild who earned quest XP that week.

Badges stay forever on your Hero window and your share card.

11Fair play

  • Everything is checked from chain data. There's nothing to self-report.
  • Trades under 0.002 ETH don't count. Your own launches don't count for buy or volume quests.
  • Quick in-and-out trades within 10 minutes are filtered out of volume quests.
  • Free chests need recent trading activity. Guild points are capped per member.
  • Materials and gear can't be transferred.

12$VQUEST & Embers

$VQUEST is the game's currency, launched on vibe/vibe. The economy follows three rules: the game never mints tokens, the game never holds anyone's tokens, and everything is checked on chain.

The Furnace: burn VQUEST, get Embers

⏳The Furnace lights up when $VQUEST graduates on vibe/vibe. Until then vibe/vibe tokens can only be bought and sold on their curve, they can't be sent anywhere, so they can't be burned yet. The Furnace shows the road to graduation live, and everything below switches on by itself the moment it graduates. The rest of the game works fully in the meantime.

At the Furnace you burn VQUEST from your own wallet (a normal transfer to the 0x…dEaD address that you sign yourself, from the game or straight from any wallet app). The indexer sees the burn and credits the same amount of Embers, usually within seconds. Burned tokens are gone for good. Embers can't be transferred.

Season rewards: only from real fees

The creator share of $VQUEST trading fees goes to the treasury wallet. Each week at most 30% of it buys VQUEST back on the market, and that's the season's reward pool: 45% guilds (top 3, split by members' quest XP), 35% Arena (top 10), 20% questers (top 10 plus a 5-winner raffle anyone can recompute). To qualify you need 5+ quests that week, and no wallet gets more than 10% of a pool. Player burns are never paid out to anyone.

Sponsored quests

Builders on vibe/vibe can book a 24h quest for a token they launched: every player gets "buy it and hold it for 6 to 48 hours", for XP and materials. Only buy-and-hold quests, two at a time, and the builder gets the list of wallets that completed it.

⚠️This is a testnet alpha. Nothing in Viberquest has monetary value, there's no airdrop or allocation promised, and $VQUEST is a game currency, not an investment. Sponsored quests are not advice to buy anything.

13FAQ

Do I have to connect my wallet?

No. You can just look at your hero by typing your address or @X handle. To play, pair instead: the game shows a tiny code amount (about 0.0002 ETH), you send exactly that to your own address on Robinhood Chain testnet, and the game unlocks a few seconds later. The ETH comes right back to you and nothing is signed on this site. Connecting a wallet is still there if you prefer it.

Is signing in safe?

You sign a plain text message with a one-time code. It's not a transaction and not an approval, so it can't move anything from your wallet.

My trade doesn't show up in a quest.

Check that it was made after you got the quest, it was worth at least 0.002 ETH and it wasn't in your own token. Trades usually land within seconds, give it a minute if the chain is busy.

When can I use the Furnace?

When $VQUEST graduates on vibe/vibe. Before that, vibe/vibe tokens can't be transferred, so they can't be burned. The Furnace shows how close graduation is.

Where can I see my rank inside my guild?

On vibercheck.xyz: open your guild, type your address or @X handle above the member table, and it shows your rank by any column.

Why can't the game see my guild?

Turn on "show me in the member list" in your vibe/vibe guild settings. The member list refreshes every 20 minutes.

Do I need to keep the game open?

No. Progress is computed on the server from the chain. Come back whenever and claim.

Is this made by vibe/vibe?

No. Viberquest is an unofficial community game built on vibe/vibe's public data by @0xHaileyy.
